No rain sound? On iPhone, check the side switch is not on silent and the volume is up. The video stays on this device. Pick it once and it is remembered.

Play on my TV

Open the Asleep in my car app on your TV. It shows a 5-letter phone code in the corner. Type it here and everything plays on the TV, with this page as the remote.
Connecting… No song playing
On the TV, use the remote to type your Apple ID and password (or use the Fire TV app on your phone as a keyboard). Press Done signing in when you're through.
While connected, Start the rain, the Mix sliders, the fade timer and the Play buttons in your song list all control the TV.

Apple Music

In Apple Music, tap the three dots on a playlist, then Share, then Copy Link. Adding a link only saves it to your list. It never interrupts what is playing.

    Sign in to Apple Music inside this player once to hear full songs. Without signing in you only get 30-second previews. Use the controls inside it to play and skip, and your device volume for the music. The sliders below only change the rain.
